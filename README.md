# Fake Busters: Deepfake Detection Comparative Analysis 🛡️🤖

## 📌 Project Overview (Work in Progress - ~75% Complete)
Developed by the **Fake Busters** team at the University of Jeddah, this project presents a rigorous comparative study of five state-of-the-art architectures designed for Deepfake detection. [cite_start]While the framework and initial evaluations are established, further optimization and final cross-solution comparisons are ongoing[cite: 671, 763].

## 🔬 Collaborative Research & Methodology
[cite_start]Our team implemented a multi-faceted approach to evaluate different algorithmic vulnerabilities across the digital media landscape[cite: 19]. We are benchmarking five distinct architectural strategies:

1.  [cite_start]**SOA Baseline (ViT-CLIP):** Evaluating hybrid architectures as a benchmark for generalizable face forgery detection[cite: 24, 61].
2.  [cite_start]**Dual-Branch Spatial & Spectral Analysis:** Combining ConvNeXt V2 with Patch-wise FFT to detect both visual and frequency-domain artifacts[cite: 20, 149].
3.  [cite_start]**Unsupervised Anomaly Detection:** Implementing Deep Autoencoders to identify manipulated deviations from pristine data distributions[cite: 21, 339].
4.  [cite_start]**Hybrid Feature Fusion:** Fusing spatial, frequency, and texture features evaluated by classical ML models (RBF SVM)[cite: 22, 469].
5.  [cite_start]**Mesoscopic Architecture Analysis:** Targeting intermediate-level visual artifacts and blending boundaries introduced by generative models[cite: 23, 624].

## 📊 Current Progress & Status
* [cite_start]**Unified Pipeline:** Completed processing over 360,000 localized facial images from FF++ and Celeb-DF v2[cite: 25, 96].
* [cite_start]**Integrity & Validation:** Standardized GPU-accelerated extraction with video-level stratification is fully implemented[cite: 101, 104].
* [cite_start]**Preliminary Results:** Promising initial accuracy rates achieved (e.g., ConvNeXt V2 at 97.39%), with ongoing hyperparameter tuning for other models[cite: 151, 394, 672, 794].
